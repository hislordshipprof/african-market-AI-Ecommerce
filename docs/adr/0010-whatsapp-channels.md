# 10. WhatsApp: customers message the store's own number; owners message the platform's

Date: 2026-10-30 · Status: accepted

Customers will be able to order through the Shopper assistant on WhatsApp (Phase 2) by messaging **the store's own WhatsApp number**, which keeps the store's brand (ADR-0003). That means each store goes through Meta business verification during onboarding. Before building, verify whether a store can keep using the WhatsApp Business app on the same number alongside the API. Store owners talk to the **Store copilot** through **one platform WhatsApp number** (Phase 1), because that is internal to the store, not customer-facing. We rejected a single platform number for customers because it would put our brand in front of the store's.

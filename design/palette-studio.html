Storefront Palette Studio

Decision made: Palm & Shea is the default Theme preset, with Cocoa & Bottle Green and Lagoon & Coral as the other two, and Night Market as the dark theme (ADR-0011). Six directions from the Dribbble, delivery-app and African-market research, each applied to a real storefront and cart. Each was revised after a critique pass with the Impeccable and Taste design skills. Pick a palette, switch light and dark, and try it under another store's brand color to see how the shared system holds up. The contrast checks are recomputed live.

Shop: browse, search by local name, quick-add
Cart: swap rules, a substitution prompt, honest fees

Store names, prices and products are examples. Product art is placeholder line drawings; real storefronts use catalog photos. Type is fixed here (Bricolage Grotesque with Figtree) so the comparison stays about color. Motion uses the timings measured from DoorDash and Instacart: 50 ms press and 200 ms release, a spring for the quantity stepper and cart count, and a 300 ms slide-up toast. All motion turns off under reduced-motion.
